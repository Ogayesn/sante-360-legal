Health data processing

Provisional version of 23 September 2026, pending legal review

What you agree to

By giving your consent, you allow us to store and process the health data you enter or import in the app, for the sole purpose of showing it to you, interpreting it against reference ranges, comparing it over time and sending you the reminders you set up.

Data concerned

  • Your lab reports (documents and results).
  • Your vital signs (blood pressure, blood glucose, weight, heart rate, temperature, oxygen saturation), including those you choose to import from Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Your health profile: medical history, treatments, allergies, height, weight.

What the app does

  • It reads your imported lab reports with an artificial intelligence service (Anthropic) that receives only the document, without your identity. You check and correct each result before it is saved.
  • It compares each result with the reference range printed by the laboratory or, failing that, with general adult reference ranges.
  • It shows general recommendations based on fixed rules written in advance.

What the app does not do

  • It does not diagnose and does not replace medical advice. If a result worries you or you have symptoms, see a doctor; in an emergency, call the emergency services.
  • It never shares your data with a doctor, employer, insurer or advertiser without action on your part.
  • It never puts health data in notifications, technical logs or web addresses.

Who can see your data

Only you. If you create a share link, the physician who opens it sees the content you chose until the link expires or is revoked; each view is counted and visible to you.

Withdrawing your consent

You can withdraw this consent at any time in Profile > My data > Withdraw my consent. You can then no longer add or change health data; existing data is kept until you delete your account. You can export it and then delete your account to erase everything.

For more information, see the privacy policy.
