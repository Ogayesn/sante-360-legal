Privacy policy

Provisional version of 23 September 2026, pending legal review

Who we are

The Santé 360 app is published by Oumar Gaye, an individual developer based in Dakar, Senegal. The full postal address is shown on the app’s Google Play listing and is available on request. We are the controller of your data.

Contact for any question about your data: gayeoumar@gmail.com. Data protection officer: no data protection officer appointed to date; requests are handled at the address above.

Our commitment

Your health data belongs to you. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and the app contains no advertising or audience-measurement tools. Nobody on our team looks at your health data in the normal course of the service.

Data we process

  • Account: email address and one-time sign-in codes.
  • Health profile, if you fill it in: date of birth, sex, height, weight, medical history, treatments, allergies.
  • Lab reports: photos or PDFs you import, results (test, value, unit, reference range), date and laboratory.
  • Prescriptions: photos or PDFs you import, prescriber, purpose, dates (prescription, end of validity, next appointment), renewals and notes. They are never sent to an automatic reading service.
  • Vital signs: blood pressure, blood glucose, weight, heart rate, temperature, oxygen saturation, entered by you or imported with your permission from Apple Health or Health Connect (read-only).
  • Reminders: reminder type and times; a medication name never appears in notifications.
  • Consents: type, version and date of each agreement or withdrawal.
  • Subscription: Premium status, product, start and end dates. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Physician sharing: expiry date and number of views of each link. The link itself is not stored in readable form.
  • Technical data: device notification token, language, technical logs without any health data, and an access log that records only identifiers (who, what, when) to protect your record.

Why and on what basis

  • Providing the service (account, record, reminders, subscription): performance of the contract you accept in the terms of use.
  • Processing your health data (lab reports, vital signs, health profile, interpretation and general recommendations): your explicit consent, collected separately in the app (GDPR Article 9(2)(a) and equivalent provisions of local laws). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Securing the service and keeping proof of consent: our legitimate interest and legal obligations.

Automatic reading of lab reports

When you import a lab report, the document (image or PDF) is sent to Anthropic's artificial intelligence service to extract the results. Only the document is sent: not your name, email or any account identifier. Anthropic may not use these documents to train its models. Anthropic keeps requests temporarily for security and compliance purposes; the exact period depends on the agreement in place.

Extracted results are only suggestions: nothing is saved to your record until you have checked and validated them. The recommendations shown come from fixed general rules written in advance; they are not generated by artificial intelligence and are not a diagnosis.

Who receives your data

  • Supabase (hosting of the database, files and server functions), in the European Union (Paris, France).
  • Anthropic (reading imported lab reports), in the United States: the document only, without any identifier.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management), in the United States: a random account identifier and your subscription status, no health data.
  • Expo (notification delivery), in the United States: the device token and a generic text without health data.
  • Apple and Google: subscription payments, notification delivery and, if you enable it, reading data from Apple Health or Health Connect on your phone.
  • A physician, only if you create a share link yourself: they see the content you chose until the link expires or is revoked.

These providers act on our instructions under data processing agreements. Transfers outside the European Union are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data and files are erased immediately from the live database, then from backups within 7 days. Proof of consent and the access log are deleted with the account no legal retention obligation applies to date. Share links expire automatically after the chosen period (1, 7 or 30 days).

Security

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Strict isolation: each account can only access its own data, enforced by the database itself.
  • On the phone: encrypted cached data, optional biometric lock, app hidden in the app switcher.
  • Access log of every change to your record and every view of a share link.

Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • export all your data from Profile > Export my data (JSON file);
  • correct your information directly in the app;
  • delete your account and all your data from Profile > Delete my account, or by following the procedure at https://ogayesn.github.io/sante-360-legal/en/account_deletion.html;
  • withdraw your consent to the processing of health data (you can then no longer add health data, and you can delete your account);
  • object to processing, request its restriction, and where the law allows, set instructions for your data after your death.

To exercise these rights or for any question: gayeoumar@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (the CDP in Senegal, the AEPD in Spain, or the authority of your country of residence).

Minimum age

The service is for adults only (18 and over). It must not be used to track a child's health.

Availability

The service is not available in France at this time.

Changes

We will inform you in the app of any significant change to this policy. If it concerns the processing of your health data, we will ask for your consent again.
