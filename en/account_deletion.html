Delete your Santé 360 account

From the app

Open Santé 360, go to Profile > Delete my account, then confirm. Your account, profile, lab reports and their files, vital signs, reminders, share links and consent history are erased immediately and permanently.

Without the app

Email gayeoumar@gmail.com from your account's email address and ask for your account to be deleted. We do it within 30 days at most and confirm the deletion by email.

What is kept

Nothing, except technical backups, which are erased within 7 days. Remember to export your data first (Profile > Export my data).

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought on the App Store or Google Play: cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings.
